# Home Inventory & Shopping List – Support

Thank you for using Home Inventory & Shopping List.

Home Inventory & Shopping List helps you organize your household inventory, manage shopping lists, track expiration dates, monitor low-stock items, and organize items by category, storage location, and store.

## Common Questions

### How do I add an inventory item?

Open the Inventory section in the app and use the Add button to create a new inventory item.

### How do I add an item to my shopping list?

Open the Shopping List section and use the Add button to add an item.

### How does expiration tracking work?

You can assign an expiration date and alert period to supported inventory items. The app can help you identify items that are approaching or have passed their expiration dates.

### How do I manage categories, stores, and storage locations?

Open Settings in the app to manage the available organizational options.
