# Privacy Policy for Home Inventory & Shopping List

**Last Updated: September 24, 2026**

Home Inventory & Shopping List is designed to help you manage your household inventory, shopping lists, expiration dates, storage locations, stores, and related information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information You Enter

Home Inventory & Shopping List allows you to enter and manage information such as:

- Inventory item names and quantities
- Shopping list items
- Categories
- Storage locations
- Stores
- Expiration dates
- Notes
- Item images or photos
- Other information you choose to enter into the app

This information is used to provide the app's inventory, shopping list, expiration tracking, notification, and related features.

## Data Storage and iCloud

App data may be stored on your device and may use Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to synchronize supported data across your Apple devices.

Data stored or synchronized using Apple's services is subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms.

The developer does not operate a separate server for collecting your inventory or shopping list information.

## Photos

The app may allow you to select or capture photos for inventory or shopping list items.

Photos you choose to associate with items are used to provide this functionality. The app does not use your photos for advertising.

## Notifications

The app may request permission to send notifications, including notifications related to expiration dates and low-stock items.

You can manage notification permissions through your device's Settings.

## Data Collection and Tracking

Home Inventory & Shopping List does not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

The developer does not sell your personal information.

## Third-Party Services

The app may use services provided by Apple, including iCloud and CloudKit, to provide app functionality.

Your use of Apple services is also subject to Apple's privacy policies and terms.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Information you create in the app remains available according to the app's functionality and your iCloud settings.

You can delete individual inventory and shopping list information using the app's available controls.

Data synchronized through iCloud may also be subject to Apple's iCloud data management and retention practices.

## Children's Privacy

Home Inventory & Shopping List is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or tracking purposes.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
